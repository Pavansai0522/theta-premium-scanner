Risk disclosure

This scanner identifies potential premium-selling setups. It does not place trades and does not predict profit. Short calls carry theoretically unlimited loss and short puts substantial downside. Options can be assigned early. Delta shifts as the underlying moves, gamma risk grows into expiration, IV changes reprice both legs, and wide markets cost real money on entry and exit. Check margin and buying-power requirements with your broker.
